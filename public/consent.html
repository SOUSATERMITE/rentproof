SMS Consent Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  rentproof.homes

How Tenants Are Added

Tenants receive SMS messages through RentProof only after their landlord adds them to the platform. Before adding a tenant's phone number, landlords confirm that they have received verbal or written consent from the tenant to receive SMS communications regarding their tenancy.

Message Types

RentProof sends the following types of SMS messages only:

  • Monthly rent payment reminders with a secure link to upload proof of payment
  • Payment receipt confirmations after a landlord verifies a payment

We do not send promotional, marketing, or advertising messages of any kind.

Message Frequency

Tenants may receive 2–4 SMS messages per month depending on their payment activity (typically one reminder and one receipt per rental period).

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to opt out of all future SMS messages from RentProof. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

Help

Reply HELP to any message to receive support contact information.

Rates & Carriers

Message and data rates may apply. RentProof is compatible with all major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy or to request removal from the platform, contact us at sousatermite@aol.com.
